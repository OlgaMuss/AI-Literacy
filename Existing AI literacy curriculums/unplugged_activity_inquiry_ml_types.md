# Unplugged Activity Inquiry: Supervised vs Unsupervised vs Reinforcement Learning

## **Objective**
Find or design an unplugged activity for 14-15 year olds that:
- Consolidates knowledge of supervised vs unsupervised vs reinforcement learning
- Includes movement/physical activity
- Lasts maximum 20 minutes
- Is engaging and age-appropriate

### **Activity Descriptions:**

- **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Movement:** Students physically move tokens/coins
- **Setup:** 5 coins on table, students take 1-2 coins alternately
- **Learning:** Students act as RL agents, learn through trial and error
- **Materials:** Tokens, game board, action cards

## **Proposed New Activities**

### **Activity 1: "ML Types Relay Race" (20 minutes)**
**Objective:** Students physically demonstrate the three types of machine learning through movement and decision-making.

**Setup:**
- Divide class into 3 teams (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement)
- Each team gets a different colored flag/badge
- Set up 3 stations around the room with different challenges

**Station 1 - Supervised Learning (5 min):**
- **Challenge:** "Teacher-Student Classification"
- **Movement:** Students move to different corners based on teacher's instructions
- **Setup:** 4 corners labeled with categories (Animals, Vehicles, Food, Colors)
- **Activity:** Teacher holds up cards, students must move to correct corner
- **Learning:** Students learn with labeled examples (supervised)

**Station 2 - Unsupervised Learning (5 min):**
- **Challenge:** "Pattern Discovery"
- **Movement:** Students move around freely, then group themselves
- **Setup:** Scatter colored shapes/objects around room
- **Activity:** Students find patterns and group similar items without instructions
- **Learning:** Students discover patterns without labels (unsupervised)

**Station 3 - Reinforcement Learning (5 min):**
- **Challenge:** "Trial and Error Navigation"
- **Movement:** Students navigate obstacle course with feedback
- **Setup:** Simple obstacle course with rewards/penalties
- **Activity:** Students try different paths, get feedback, improve
- **Learning:** Students learn through trial and error with rewards (reinforcement)

**Final Discussion (5 min):**
- Teams share what they learned about their ML type
- Compare and contrast the three approaches
- Discuss real-world applications

**Materials Needed:**
- Colored flags/badges (3 colors)
- Category cards for supervised learning
- Colored shapes/objects for unsupervised learning
- Obstacle course materials (cones, tape, rewards)

### **Activity 2: "ML Types Charades" (15 minutes)**
**Objective:** Students act out scenarios representing different ML types.

**Setup:**
- Prepare scenario cards for each ML type
- Students work in small groups (3-4 people)

**Scenarios:**
**Supervised Learning:**
- "Email spam filter learning from labeled emails"
- "Face recognition system being trained with photos"
- "Voice assistant learning to understand commands"

**Unsupervised Learning:**
- "Music streaming service grouping similar songs"
- "Shopping website grouping similar customers"
- "Social media finding trending topics"

**Reinforcement Learning:**
- "Self-driving car learning to park"
- "Game AI learning to play chess"
- "Robot learning to walk"

**Activity:**
1. Groups pick a scenario card (5 min prep)
2. Groups perform their scenario (2 min each)
3. Class guesses which ML type is being demonstrated
4. Discussion of why each scenario fits its ML type

**Materials Needed:**
- Scenario cards
- Timer
- Props (optional)

### **Activity 3: "ML Types Sorting Challenge" (20 minutes)**
**Objective:** Students physically sort real-world examples into ML categories.

**Setup:**
- Large poster boards labeled "Supervised," "Unsupervised," "Reinforcement"
- Cards with real-world AI examples
- Students work in teams of 4-5

**Example Cards:**
- "Netflix recommending movies" (Supervised)
- "Google Photos grouping similar faces" (Unsupervised)
- "AlphaGo learning to play Go" (Reinforcement)
- "Email spam detection" (Supervised)
- "Customer segmentation for marketing" (Unsupervised)
- "Self-driving car learning to navigate" (Reinforcement)
- "Image recognition for medical diagnosis" (Supervised)
- "Finding patterns in social media data" (Unsupervised)
- "Game AI learning to play video games" (Reinforcement)

**Activity:**
1. Teams receive 9-12 example cards
2. Teams discuss and sort cards into categories (10 min)
3. Teams present their sorting with explanations (5 min each)
4. Class discusses any disagreements or corrections (5 min)

**Materials Needed:**
- Large poster boards (3)
- Example cards (9-12 per team)
- Markers
- Timer

- ✅ 20 minutes duration
- ✅ Physical movement (handling tokens)
- ✅ Age-appropriate for 14-15 year olds
- ✅ Reinforcement learning focus
- ❌ Only covers reinforcement learning, not all three types

### **Recommended New Activity:**
**"ML Types Relay Race"** - This activity best meets all requirements:
- ✅ 20 minutes duration
- ✅ Physical movement and engagement
- ✅ Covers all three ML types
- ✅ Age-appropriate and engaging
- ✅ Consolidates knowledge through comparison

### **Implementation Notes:**
1. **Preparation:** Set up stations in advance, ensure clear instructions
2. **Timing:** Use timers to keep activities on track
3. **Debriefing:** Allow time for discussion and reflection
4. **Adaptation:** Can be modified for different class sizes
5. **Assessment:** Use final discussion to check understanding

### **Alternative Approach:**
If time is limited, use **"ML Types Charades"** as it's shorter (15 min) and still covers all three types with movement and engagement.
