# 💻Using AI on the Create Task {#💻using-ai-on-the-create-task}

## Understanding AI Code Generators

In the landscape of computer science education, AI code generators stand as a testament to the rapid advancements in technology. To effectively incorporate these tools into the AP CSP curriculum, it is essential to first understand what they are and how they function.

**What are AI code generators?**

AI code generators are sophisticated software systems that use artificial intelligence to assist in writing, optimizing, and troubleshooting code. These tools have been trained on vast repositories of programming data, enabling them to suggest code snippets, complete coding tasks, and even debug existing code. They serve not just as assistants but also as learning aids, providing students with real-time examples of coding practices and solutions.

**How do AI code generators work?**

These generators function by analyzing the input provided by the user, such as a description of a task or a partially completed code segment. Utilizing their extensive training, they generate relevant code suggestions or solutions. This process involves complex algorithms that understand programming languages, coding syntax, and sometimes even the context or the objective of the given task.

**Benefits in a Learning Environment**

* AI code generators can offer multiple ways to solve a problem, exposing students to a variety of coding styles and techniques.  
* They provide instant feedback and suggestions, allowing students to learn and correct their mistakes in real time.  
* With the safety net of AI assistance, students might feel more encouraged to experiment and try complex coding tasks.  
* These tools can help students overcome stumbling blocks in coding, making the learning process more efficient and less frustrating.

**Limitations and Considerations**

While AI code generators are powerful tools, it's important to recognize their limitations. They should not replace fundamental understanding or manual coding. There's a risk of students becoming overly reliant on these tools, which can hinder the development of problem-solving skills and a deep understanding of programming concepts. Therefore, it's crucial for you to guide students in using these tools judiciously, ensuring that they complement, rather than replace, traditional learning methods.

## Guidelines for Using AI Code Generators in the Classroom

Here are some key guidelines to help you make the most of these innovative tools in a classroom setting:

1. **Set Clear Learning Objectives**   
   * Clearly define how and why AI code generators are being used. Ensure that their use aligns with the learning objectives of the course, particularly focusing on the AP CSP Create task.  
   * Communicate to students that these tools are meant to supplement their learning, not replace the need for understanding the fundamentals of coding and problem-solving.  
2. **Foster Ethical Use**   
   * Emphasize the importance of academic honesty. Students should use AI tools for assistance, but their final submissions must be their original work, reflecting their understanding and effort.  
   * Teach students to acknowledge any assistance received from AI tools, fostering transparency and ethical use of technology.  
3. **Encourage Critical Engagement**   
   * Encourage students to critically assess the code generated by AI. They should understand why a certain solution is proposed and be able to identify any limitations or errors in the AI-generated code.  
   * Use AI tools as a means to enhance problem-solving skills. Students should first attempt to solve problems independently before seeking AI assistance.  
4. **Balanced Approach to Coding Tasks**   
   * Ensure students engage in manual coding to strengthen their coding skills and deepen their understanding of programming concepts.  
   * Utilize AI tools for complex or challenging tasks where students might benefit from additional guidance or for exploring different approaches to a problem.

## Integrating AI Tools into the Create Task

Here are some strategies for effectively incorporating AI tools into the Create task:

1. **Understanding the Create Task**   
   * Begin by ensuring that students fully understand the requirements and objectives of the Create task. Clarify the expectations around complexity, creativity, and originality.  
   * Reinforce the key concepts and skills that the Create task aims to assess, such as algorithm development, abstraction, and programming proficiency.  
2. **Role of AI in Idea Generation**   
   * Encourage students to use AI tools for brainstorming and exploring different ideas. AI can suggest various approaches to a problem, helping to spark creativity and innovation.  
   * Use AI to help students assess the feasibility of their ideas, providing insights into what's programmatically possible and guiding them in setting realistic goals.  
3. **AI-Assisted Coding**   
   * Students can use AI tools to help draft initial versions of their code. This can be particularly useful for more complex or unfamiliar aspects of their project.  
   * AI suggestions can be used to refine and optimize code, ensuring efficiency and adherence to best practices.  
4. **Troubleshooting and Debugging**   
   * AI tools can assist in identifying and explaining errors in code, providing an additional layer of support for students.  
   * Encourage students to use AI-generated suggestions as one of several strategies for debugging their code, emphasizing the importance of understanding the underlying issues.  
5. **Enhancing Code Quality**   
   * Use AI tools to conduct preliminary code reviews, offering suggestions for improvement on aspects like readability, structure, and documentation.  
   * AI can introduce students to industry-standard best practices, which they can learn from and apply in their own coding.  
6. **Reflective Documentation**   
   * Encourage students to document their use of AI tools, reflecting on how these tools influenced their approach, design decisions, and problem-solving strategies.  
   * This documentation can also serve as a means for students to articulate their learning journey, including how they overcame challenges and what they learned.

## Monitoring and Assessment

Here are strategies for you to monitor and assess the integration of AI tools in the classroom:

1. **Tracking Student Progress**   
   * Conduct periodic check-ins with students to discuss their use of AI tools and progress on the Create task. This allows for early identification of any over-reliance on AI.  
   * Set clear milestones for the Create task and monitor students' progress towards these goals, ensuring that AI tools are used appropriately at each stage.  
2. **Evaluating Ethical Use**   
   * Encourage students to be transparent about their use of AI tools. This includes acknowledging the assistance they received and differentiating between AI-generated code and their own work.  
   * Perform random checks to ensure that students are complying with ethical guidelines, such as not presenting AI-generated code as entirely their own work.  
3. **Assessing Understanding**   
   * Ask students to explain the logic behind the code they submit, especially sections where AI assistance was utilized. This can help assess their understanding of the concepts.  
   * Conduct brief oral examinations where students discuss their project and the role AI played in it. This provides insight into their comprehension and application of learning.  
4. **Quality of Code**   
   * Organize code review sessions where students review and provide feedback on each other's code. This encourages a deeper understanding and refinement of coding skills.  
   * Compare the quality of student code against established coding standards and best practices to assess proficiency and skill development.  
5. **Reflective Practice**   
   * Have students maintain journals or logs where they reflect on their use of AI tools, the challenges faced, and how they overcame them. This promotes self-assessment and critical thinking.  
   * Provide feedback on these reflective entries to guide students’ understanding and encourage deeper engagement with the learning process.
