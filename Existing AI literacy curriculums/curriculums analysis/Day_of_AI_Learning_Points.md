# Day of AI - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** MIT
- **Format:** 6 classroom-ready lessons
- **Target Age:** Middle/high school
- **Focus:** Chatbots, LLMs, responsible AI use
- **Unique Strengths:** Best explanation of LLM token-by-token generation, classroom AI norms development, human feedback layers

## Learning Points Coverage (Depth ≥2)

### LLM Mechanics (Depth 3)
1. **Word-by-word generation** - Explicit example: "The boy walked his..." showing token-by-token progression
2. **Training process detailed** - Word chunks → NN predicts → check against actual → update weights → billions of iterations (with basketball hoop slide)
3. **Three training layers** - Language → conversation → human-preferred conversation (RLHF implicit)
4. **Billions of iterations** - Explicit stat on training scale

### Chatbots & NLP (Depth 2-3)
1. **Chatbots** (Depth 3) - Full curriculum focus on conversational AI
2. **Chatbots don't understand** (Depth 3) - Explicit: responses don't always fit, guessing from patterns
3. **LLM token prediction** (Depth 3) - Detailed explanation of probability-based next-word prediction
4. **NLP field** (Depth 2) - Chatbots use NLP for language understanding

### Limitations & Verification (Depth 2-3)
1. **Hallucinations** (Depth 3) - Vocabulary and Lesson 2 focus on false confident outputs
2. **Interrogating the internet** (Depth 3) - Lesson 2 core: asking many questions, verifying with multiple sources
3. **Source evaluation** (Depth 3) - Worksheet with riot vs demonstration example, credibility/tone/POV analysis
4. **Echo chambers** (Depth 3) - Definition and discussion of hearing agreeable ideas only
5. **Don't trust first result** (Depth 2) - Optional extension on multiple source checking
6. **Chatbot limitations** (Depth 2) - Failed conversations example
7. **AI doesn't think/feel** (Depth 3) - Explicit point throughout

### Human-AI Interaction (Depth 3)
1. **Using AI for school** - Lesson 4 full focus: research tasks step table with benefits/challenges
2. **Classroom AI norms** - Lesson 4: groups draft 4-6 guidelines, gallery walk, consensus, living document
3. **AI and human connection** - Lesson 3 full lesson: daily connection chart timeline, T-chart of strengthen/erode examples
4. **Daily connection patterns** - Connection chart with sample Alexa row: who (family/friends/teachers), how (face-to-face/texting/gaming/AI), feelings

### Bias & Ethics (Depth 2-3)
1. **Bias definition** (Depth 3) - Explicit definition, internet bias, human rater bias with Duolingo example
2. **Sources of bias** (Depth 2) - Internet bias, who collects data
3. **Algorithmic bias** (Depth 3) - Chatbots and bias, Lesson 2 focus

### Other Topics (Depth 2)
1. **AI definition** - Intro with video, discussion
2. **AI vs non-AI** - Brief discussion
3. **Everyday AI applications** - Classroom discussion
4. **Algorithm definition** - Vocabulary term
5. **More data doesn't fix bias** - Implicit in discussions
6. **Generative AI** - ChatGPT classroom focus
7. **Benefits and harms** - Discussed throughout
8. **Privacy** - Discussed in norms context
9. **Quick, Draw!** - Mentioned as example
10. **LLMs vs search** - Brief mention
11. **Exit tickets** - Various lessons for reflection
