# Toddle AI 101 - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** Toddle
- **Format:** 8 scaffolded lessons with worksheets
- **Target Age:** Middle/high school
- **Focus:** Scaffolding routines, thinking frameworks, responsible AI use
- **Unique Strengths:** Only depth 3 on SAMR (AI edition), Gray Area Sort gradient, Circle of Perspectives, FAB check framework

## 8-Lesson Structure
1. Introduction to AI
2. FAB Check (Fairness, Accuracy, Bias)
3. Creativity with AI
4. AI as Research Assistant
5. Circle of Perspectives
6. SAMR: AI Edition
7. Making Thinking Visible
8. Gray Area Sort

## Learning Points Coverage (Depth ≥2)

### Critical Thinking Frameworks (Depth 3) - UNIQUE
1. **FAB check** - Fairness, Accuracy, Bias evaluation framework (Lesson 2 core tool)
2. **Gray Area Sort** - Gradient spectrum for debatable AI uses: totally ok → questionable (Lesson 8 full activity with post-its, class vote)
3. **Circle of Perspectives** - Stepping into different viewpoints: student, parent, developer, principal (Lesson 5 full routine with debate scenarios: AI photos, influencers, monitoring, voice/face copying)

### Collaboration Frameworks (Depth 3) - UNIQUE
1. **SAMR (AI edition)** - Simple help, A little better, Make together, Really new (Lesson 6: detailed levels, bus stops activity)
2. **Essential agreements** - When/how to use AI on assignments (Lessons 6+8 with gradient decisions)

### Metacognition & Reflection (Depth 2-3)
1. **How you learn with vs without AI** (Depth 3) - Lessons 6-8 throughout, when helps vs hinders
2. **Metacognition** (Depth 2) - Throughout all lessons
3. **Making thinking visible** (Depth 3) - Lesson 7 worksheets and exit tickets on AI role
4. **AI in creative process** (Depth 3) - Lesson 7: full focus on transparency beyond citations

### Problem Solving & Decision Making (Depth 3)
1. **Analyzing risks/benefits** - Lesson 8 focus with new assignment decisions
2. **Informed decision-making** - Throughout

### AI Fundamentals (Depth 2)
1. **AI definition** - Lesson 1: algorithms + weights, video
2. **AI vs non-AI** - Lesson 1 examples
3. **Everyday AI** - Lesson 1 exploration
4. **Generative AI** - Lesson 1 intro
5. **AI literacy goal** - Course objective stated

### Human-AI Interaction (Depth 2)
1. **Using AI for school** - Lessons 6-8 focus
2. **Authorial agency** - Lesson 6 collaboration emphasis
3. **Essential agreements** (Depth 2) - Multiple lessons

### Creativity (Depth 2)
1. **Supporting creativity** - Lesson 3 focus
2. **Human advantages** (Depth 2) - Throughout lessons

### Perspectives & Awareness (Depth 2)
1. **Multiple perspectives** - Lesson 5 roles
2. **Emotional intelligence** (Depth 2) - Lesson 5 awareness focus
3. **Self/social awareness** (Depth 2) - Lesson 5 debates

### Benefits & Harms (Depth 2)
1. **AI benefits** - Lesson 1, projects
2. **AI harms** - Lesson 1, awareness

### Communication (Depth 3)
1. **Communicating AI use** - Describe when/how influenced process (not just citations)

### Activities & Routines (Depth 2-3)
1. **One Word Story** (Depth 3) - Lesson 1: collaborative story-building comparing to LLM generation
2. **T-chart** (Depth 2) - Lesson 1: AI uses + questions, revisit at end
3. **Take a Stand** (Depth 2) - Lesson 1: helpful vs harmful with reasons
4. **Exit tickets** (Depth 3) - Throughout all lessons for reflection
5. **Bus stops** (Depth 3) - Lesson 6: rotating groups brainstorm at stations
6. **Gallery walk** - Implicit in activities
7. **Personas for writing** (Depth 2) - Worksheets

### Assessment
1. **Certificate of completion** (Depth 2) - After assessment with pass threshold

- Exit tickets for each lesson

## Total Learning Points with Depth ≥2: ~30 points

## Depth 3 Points: ~10 points
Focus areas: Scaffolding routines (SAMR, Circle of Perspectives, Gray Area), critical thinking frameworks (FAB), reflection tools

## Coverage Gaps (Extensive - by design)
- All technical content (ML, NN, CV, NLP mechanics)
- Reinforcement learning
- Environmental impact
- Privacy technical details
- Autonomous vehicles
- GANs architecture
- Decision trees
- Bias technical details
- Indigenous perspectives
- Coding/programming
- Most hands-on tools

## Pedagogical Approach
- **Scaffolding-focused:** Thinking routines and frameworks over technical content
- Discussion protocols (Circle of Perspectives)
- Gradient thinking (Gray Area Sort, not binary)
- Metacognitive reflection (exit tickets, worksheets)
- Collaborative routines (bus stops, gallery walk)
- Making thinking visible emphasis
- Assessment with certificate
- 8-lesson progression building on prior lessons
- **Philosophy:** Teach students HOW to think about AI, not just WHAT AI is
