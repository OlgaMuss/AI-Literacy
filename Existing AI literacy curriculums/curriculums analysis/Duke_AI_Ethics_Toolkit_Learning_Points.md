# Duke AI Ethics Toolkit - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** Duke University
- **Format:** Critical toolkit modules (11 extracted files)
- **Target Age:** High school / undergraduate
- **Focus:** Critical perspective on AI harms, systemic issues, power dynamics
- **Unique Strengths:** Only depth ≥2 on tech oligarchy critique, most critical/activist framing

## Pedagogical Approach
- **Critical pedagogy focus:** Activist/skeptical framing
- Glossary of critical terms
- Debating activities
- Manifesto creation (Anti-AI)
- Disconnect experiments
- Emphasis on systemic problems:
  - Corporate concentration
  - Hidden/underpaid labor
  - Access inequality
  - Monoculture risks
  - Surveillance
  - Copyright theft
- Less on "how AI works," more on "who benefits, who's harmed"
- Toolkit format: modular topics for integration
- Social justice lens throughout
