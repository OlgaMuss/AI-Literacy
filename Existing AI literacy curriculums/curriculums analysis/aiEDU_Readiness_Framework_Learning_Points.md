# aiEDU AI Readiness Framework v2.0 - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** aiEDU
- **Format:** K-12 framework with AI Snapshots, SmartTeach, Indigenous AI, Future of Work
- **Target Age:** K-5, 6-8, 9-12 progressions
- **Focus:** Human Advantage Domain + AI Competencies, extensive real-world scenarios
- **Unique Strengths:** Only curriculum with depth 3 on Indigenous AI (5+ points), AI Readiness Framework, 30+ AI Snapshots with domain-specific applications

## Framework Structure
- **Human Advantage Domain:** Emotional intelligence, creativity, critical thinking, metacognition that AI cannot easily replicate
- **AI Competencies:** Understanding, evaluating, using AI responsibly
- **Progressions:** Age-appropriate from K-5 to 9-12
