# Common Sense Media AI Literacy - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** Common Sense Media
- **Format:** Introductory slides and lessons
- **Target Age:** Middle school
- **Focus:** AI basics, benefits/harms balance, responsible use introduction
- **Unique Strengths:** Accessible entry point, balanced benefits/harms framing

## Learning Points Coverage (Depth ≥2)

### AI Fundamentals (Depth 2)
1. **AI definition** - Definition video plus generative AI focus
2. **Everyday AI applications** - Warm-up discussion

### Generative AI (Depth 2)
1. **Generative AI overview** - Definition, examples
2. **Real tools** - ChatGPT and other examples

### Benefits & Harms (Depth 2)
1. **AI benefits** - Helpful category in Lesson 1: efficiency, creativity, learning support
2. **AI harms** - Harmful category in Lesson 1: bias, misinformation, privacy risks
3. **Benefits comprehensive** - Warm-up activity
4. **Harms comprehensive** - Slide + lesson coverage

### Bias & Fairness (Depth 2)
1. **Bias in training data** - Discussed in slides and lessons
2. **Algorithmic bias** - Lesson focus

### Deepfakes & Misinformation (Depth 2)
1. **Misinformation** - Harm category with discussion
2. **Decrease misinformation** - Discussed
3. **Source evaluation** - Misinformation lesson

### Privacy (Depth 2)
1. **How AI is trained** - Data collection lesson
2. **Personal data** - How collected/used

### Human-AI Interaction (Depth 2)
1. **AI and human connection** - Friendship impacts lesson
2. **Chatbots human-like design** - Lesson content

### Other Topics (Depth 1-2)
1. **More data doesn't fix bias** - Slides mention
2. **Garbage in, garbage out** - Implicit
3. **Data choice affects outcomes** - How AI learns
4. **Recommendation algorithms** - Algorithms lesson
5. **Plagiarism** - Full lesson
6. **Copyright infringement** - Lesson coverage
7. **Chatbots mentioned** - Lesson context

## Total Learning Points with Depth ≥2: ~20 points

## Depth 3 Points: 0
**Note:** All coverage at depth 1-2 (introductory/exploratory, no deep activities)

## Coverage Gaps (Extensive - Introductory Curriculum)
- All technical depth (ML, NN, CV, NLP mechanics)
- Reinforcement learning
- Environmental impact
- Ethical frameworks (matrices, EU guidelines)
- Autonomous vehicles
- Indigenous perspectives
- Prompt engineering
- GANs architecture
- Decision trees
- Programming/coding
- Hands-on tools
- Most real-world case studies

## Pedagogical Approach
- **Entry-level focus**
- Discussion-based
- Slide presentations
- Balanced framing (benefits AND harms)
- Age-appropriate simplification
- Warm-up activities
- Brief lessons
- No hands-on coding
- Focus on awareness over technical skills

## Purpose
Acts as gentle introduction to AI literacy before deeper curricula. Good for:
- Initial exposure
- Building vocabulary
- Starting conversations
- Younger or less technical audiences
