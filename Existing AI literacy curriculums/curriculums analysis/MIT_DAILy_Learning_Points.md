# MIT DAILy Curriculum - Learning Points Analysis

## Curriculum Overview
- **Developer:** MIT Media Lab
- **Target Age:** High school
- **Focus:** Ethical AI design, algorithms as opinions, stakeholder analysis
- **Unique Strengths:** Only depth 3 on algorithms as opinions, ethical matrices tool, classification vs generation contrast, decision trees reflect builder bias

## Learning Points Coverage (Depth ≥2)

### Ethics & Design (Depth 3)
1. **Algorithms as opinions** - Lesson 0.2 core: different goals/stakeholders lead to different designs
2. **Ethical matrices** - Lessons 0.3, 3.8: PB&J, YouTube redesign mapping stakeholders→values→goals
3. **Stakeholders** - Multiple lessons: who's affected, why they care, optimized vs not optimized
4. **Algorithm definition** - PB&J activity: list goals, identify stakeholders
5. **Design affects different groups** - Ethical matrices throughout

### Machine Learning (Depth 2-3)
1. **Supervised learning** (Depth 3) - Core Unit 1 with multiple lessons
2. **Labeled data** (Depth 3) - Core concept with examples
3. **Training vs test data** (Depth 2) - Neural networks lesson
4. **Data diversity vs quantity** (Depth 3) - Explicit Lesson 1.1: more data improves but diversity fixes bias
5. **Classification vs generation** (Depth 3) - Full Lesson 1.3: color mixing platform, examples, practice distinguishing
6. **Bias mitigation** (Depth 3) - Re-curating datasets explicit lesson with justification
7. **Algorithmic bias** (Depth 3) - Lesson 0.5 + Unit 1.1 + multiple units
8. **Sources of bias** (Depth 2) - Data diversity emphasis

### Decision Trees (Depth 3)
1. **Structure: root/decision/leaf nodes** - PastaLand detailed lesson with diagram labeling
2. **Features for classification** - PastaLand feature identification
3. **Building trees iteratively** - Detailed multi-step process
4. **Trees reflect builder bias** - Explicit learning objective on prior experience influencing design

### Neural Networks (Depth 3)
1. **Architecture: input/hidden/output layers** - Lesson 1.2: diagram labeling, structure description
2. **Neurons, weights, layers** - Diagram labeling activity
3. **Weights adjusted in training** - Training process steps with backpropagation
4. **Feedforward, evaluation, backpropagation** - Explicit three-step labeling activity

### Generative AI (Depth 2-3)
1. **GANs: generator and discriminator** (Depth 3) - Unit 2.1-2.2: components, competition, adversarial training detailed
2. **GANs generate multiple media** (Depth 3) - Exploring GANs activity: photos, paintings, poetry, music, jokes
3. **Distinguishing GAN art from human** (Depth 3) - GANs or Not? activity
4. **Generative AI overview** (Depth 2) - Stories unit
5. **Real tools** (Depth 2) - Various units cover applications

### Deepfakes & Misinformation (Depth 2-3)
1. **Deepfakes** (Depth 3) - Unit 2.5 full lesson
2. **7 clues to spot deepfakes** (Depth 3) - Hair, eyes, side profile, emotions, teeth, background, asymmetry
3. **Deepfake harms** (Depth 2) - Consequences lesson on blackmail, defamation, election manipulation
4. **Misinformation vs disinformation** (Depth 3) - Unit focus with intent distinction
5. **Misinformation spreads faster** (Depth 2) - Lesson description states research
6. **Fact vs opinion** (Depth 2) - Lesson content with loaded language
7. **Decrease misinformation** (Depth 2) - Propose solutions activity
8. **Source evaluation** (Depth 2) - Misinformation lesson emphasis

### Computer Vision (Depth 2)
1. **Computer vision overview** - Various units
2. **Real applications** - Multiple contexts

### AI Careers (Depth 2-3)
1. **Jobs transformation** (Depth 3) - Careers unit: Career Daydream, roadmap, Holland types, STEM diversity
2. **AI careers multidisciplinary** (Depth 2) - Career unit coverage
3. **Future jobs unknown** (Depth 2) - Career roadmap with resilience/adaptability

### Other Topics (Depth 2)
1. **AI definition** - Three components framework
2. **AI or Not?** - Activity
3. **Computer perception** - AI4K12 standard incorporated
4. **Everyday AI** - Journal activities
5. **Route finding: graphs and trees** - Lesson with data structures
6. **Breadth-first search** - Activity
7. **Benefits and harms** - Consequences lessons
8. **AI art debate** - Full lesson on authorship
9. **Copyright ownership** - Explicit lesson activity with rule proposals
10. **Plagiarism lesson** - Full coverage
11. **Using AI to support creativity** - AI art/stories unit
12. **Explore AI journal** - Documentation activity
13. **Unanticipated consequences** - Lesson 2.3: AI effects on social/cultural/economic/political
14. **Cultural bias in vision** - Lesson emphasis

## Projects
- **PB&J Algorithm** - Write algorithm, identify stakeholders, list goals
- **YouTube Redesign** - Culminating project with goals, bias reduction, three dataset types, flowcharts/pseudocode, ethical matrix, feedback incorporation
- **PastaLand Decision Trees** - Build trees with features and cross-testing
- **GANs Exploration** - Create content with GAN tools
- **GANs or Not?** - Distinguish AI from human art
- **Original Story** - Using GAN tools for text and images
- **Explore AI Journal** - Throughout curriculum
