**How to use**

We tried to make the case study interactive with videos and reflection
activities. The case study and something that you can do as a whole
class or students can work on independently. However, there is still a
bit a writing. Feel free to select 1-2 reflection activities to make
this more manageable.

**Feedback:** I created this in Google Slides to reduce the cognitive
load of looking at a lot of text instead of creating a worksheet for
this. Let me know how it works for you. Again, feel free to adapt this
to what will work with your students and your teaching preferences.

**Additional Case Studies:** I am still working on another case study on
safety of self-driving cars but this one will require more reading
because it is article based so it is taking a little more time to
develop. For those doing the case study next week, you will have a few
options to give the students.

**Data Collection:** We are curious about how this style of case study
works for your students. Please have students record their answers to
whichever prompts you use.

**What Are the Basic Elements of Attractive Nuisance?**

- A potentially dangerous condition exists on the property.

- The landowner created or maintained the potential hazard.

- The landowner should have known the condition would attract children.

- The landowner should have known the condition could harm children.
