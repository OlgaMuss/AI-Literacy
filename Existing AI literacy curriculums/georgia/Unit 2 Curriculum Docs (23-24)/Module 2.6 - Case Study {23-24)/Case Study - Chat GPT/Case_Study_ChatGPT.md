**Part 3 - In-Class Debate**

Pick a debate topic, e.g., "ChatGPT is bad (i.e., destructive) for
humanity", or "The benefits of ChatGPT substantially outweigh the
risks".

**Debate Structure**

**Opening Statements**

- (2 min) Team Affirmative (For) - 1st Speaker

- (2 min) Team Negative (Against) - 1st Speaker

**Rebuttals**

- (5 min) Teams prepare rebuttals to opening statements

- (2 min) Team Affirmative Rebuttal - 2nd Speaker (2 min) Team Negative
  Rebuttal - 2nd Speaker

**Response to Rebuttals**

- (3 min) Teams prepare response to rebuttal statements

- (2 min) Team Affirmative Response to Rebuttal - 3rd Speaker

- (2 min) Team Negative Response to Rebuttal - 3rd Speaker

- Closing Statements

- (5 min) Teams prepare Closing Statements

- (2 min) Team Affirmative Closing Statement - 4th Speaker

- (2 min) Team Negative Response closing Statement - 4th Speaker

- CLASS VOTE

- Final reflection statement (on debate form)

- Turn in Debate form

**Debate Rules**

> 1\. Stick to the structure!
>
> 2\. Do not talk when it\'s not your turn. No interjections or
> interruptions.
>
> 3\. Stay on topic.
>
> 4\. No inappropriate language.
>
> 5\. No resorting to personal attacks.

**Debate Goals**

- Impactful statements

- Data-backed evidence

- Eloquent delivery

- Understanding of the topic
