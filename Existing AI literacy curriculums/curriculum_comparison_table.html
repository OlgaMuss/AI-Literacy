  1. AI4K12 Initiative began in 2018 (NSF Grant DRL-1846073); major publication "Artificial Intelligence Thinking in K-12" by Touretzky & Gardner-McCune published in 2021. Source: ai4k12.org

  3. Berlin KI-Aufträge curriculum materials initially developed January 2021, with ongoing updates through 2024. Source: KI-Aufträge.pdf, Berlin Senatsverwaltung für Bildung, Jugend und Familie

  5. aiEDU: The AI Education Project unveiled curriculum and learning products on December 11, 2024. Source: PR Newswire, December 14, 2024
  6. Georgia AI4GA co-design process presented at SIGCSE March 2023; public announcement May 21, 2024. Source: Georgia Tech News
  7. ENARIS curriculum associated with Roteco research published August 25, 2022 in Frontiers in Education. Source: EPFL Infoscience
  8. "AI in My Life" programme published at WebSci '21 (13th ACM Web Science Conference), June 21-25, 2021. Source: DORAS DCU Repository
  9. MIT AI Ethics Education Curriculum: Original version published August 2019 by Blakeley H. Payne; expanded project-based curricula published through 2022 including "AI + Ethics Curricula for Middle School Youth" (International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, August 2022). Source: MIT Center and MIT Media Lab
  10. Duke Learning Innovation AI Ethics Toolkit launched Spring 2025. Source: Duke LILE
  11. AI Pedagogy Project launched November 16, 2023 by Harvard's metaLAB. Source: Berkman Klein Center
  12. Toddle AI launched in 2023; AI 2.0 announced January 31, 2024. Source: Toddle Blog
  13. Day of AI "Brain Behind the Bot" curriculum developed 2025 by MIT Media Lab (Isabella Pu, Graduate Research Assistant) and Day of AI team. Covers chatbots, NLP, LLMs, and hallucinations for ages 12-18. Source: Day of AI
