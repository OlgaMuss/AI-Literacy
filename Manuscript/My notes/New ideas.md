Problems with curriculum as identified by international bureau of education:
- Outdated content does not reflect contemporary realities or future needs. —> content alignement tools
- Weak alignment between curriculum, pedagogy, and assessment. —> internal alignement tool
- Limited inclusion of local knowledge, environmental education, and social-emotional learning.—> local graounding assessment
- Insufficient capacity and resources to design, implement, and review curricula effectively. —> AI can help: specifically because it is able to conseider simulatneously many variables.
