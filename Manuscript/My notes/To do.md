--> strategies:
Short-term programs (<2 months) rarely create lasting change. Longer interventions (3+ months) are associated with significant, sustained improvements in DL (Li, Zheng & Xie, 2025; Timotheou et al., 2023).

Evidence:

Multi-year programs (e.g., Estonia’s ProgeTiger) are more effective than short-term workshops (OECD, 2023).
Spaced, repetitive learning reinforces skills and supports long-term retention (Van Dijk & Van Deursen, 2014).
