## 📥 Download Instructions

### For Open Access Papers:
1. Click the link
2. Download PDF directly
3. Save to: `/Literature/Literature on HandsOn/[FirstAuthor_Year_ShortTitle].pdf`

### For Papers Behind Paywalls:
1. Check if your institution has access
2. Try Google Scholar "PDF" links
3. Check ResearchGate author uploads
4. Use inter-library loan if necessary
5. Contact authors directly (many provide upon request)

### Naming Convention:
`[FirstAuthor]_[Year]_[ShortTitle].pdf`

Examples:
- `Zhong_2022_Taxonomy_STEM.pdf`
- `IKEA_effect_robots_2024.pdf`
- `Ouyang_2024_MetaAnalysis_Robotics.pdf`
